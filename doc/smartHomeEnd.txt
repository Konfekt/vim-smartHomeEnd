VIM-SMARTHOMEEND                                   *smarthomeend*

Vim plug-in makes the `<Home/End>` keys cycle through the starts/endings of
the screen rows spanned by the current line in Normal and Visual Mode.
In Insert Mode, the read-line bindings `<Ctrl-A/E>` are remapped instead.
Suggested remappings:
>
    nmap H <Home>
    xmap H <Home>
    nmap L <End>
    xmap L <End>
<

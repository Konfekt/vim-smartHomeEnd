INTRODUCTION                                     *smarthomeend* *vim-smarthomeend*

This plug-in makes the <Home> and <End> keys cycle through the starts and ends
of the screen rows spanned by the current line in Normal and Visual mode.
In Insert mode, the readline bindings CTRL-A and CTRL-E are remapped instead.

- <Home> alternates between the first non-blank character and the first
  column of the screen row; repeated presses move up through the screen rows
  of a wrapped line.
- <End> moves to the end of the screen row; repeated presses move down
  through the screen rows of a wrapped line.
- In Insert mode, CTRL-E on the last screen row puts the cursor after the
  last character. At the end of the line, or while the completion popup menu
  is visible, CTRL-E keeps its built-in meaning |i_CTRL-E|.

If 'wrap' is off, the buffer-line motions |^|, |0| and |$| are used instead of
the screen-row motions |g^|, |g0| and |g$|.

==============================================================================
MAPPINGS                                               *smarthomeend-mappings*

Default mappings are only created if the keys are not mapped already.

                                                  *<Plug>(SmartHomeEnd-Home)*
<Plug>(SmartHomeEnd-Home)
        Smart Home. Mapped to <Home> in Normal and Visual mode and to CTRL-A
        in Insert mode.

                                                   *<Plug>(SmartHomeEnd-End)*
<Plug>(SmartHomeEnd-End)
        Smart End. Mapped to <End> in Normal and Visual mode and to CTRL-E in
        Insert mode.

Suggested remappings:
>
    nmap H <Home>
    xmap H <Home>
    nmap L <End>
    xmap L <End>
<
